Who We Are

Driven by Compassion,
Powered by Youth

We are a collective of young volunteers from Kerala who believe small acts of service, repeated across communities, can light up thousands of lives.

Since2019

Our Story

A spark that became a movement

Moonlit Foundation began in 2019 when a small group of college students in Kozhikode organized a single blood donation camp for a stranger in need. That one weekend showed us how much a handful of committed young people can do — and how many more were waiting for a way to help.

Today we are a registered youth-led nonprofit with volunteers across Kerala, working in blood donation, community welfare, disaster relief, environmental action and youth development. Our belief has never changed: real change starts with showing up for your own community.

Join Our Journey

Our Mission

To mobilize the energy of young people into organized, compassionate action — saving lives through blood donation, standing with families in crisis, and caring for the environment we all share.

Our Vision

A Kerala where no emergency goes unanswered, no family faces hardship alone, and every young person sees service to their community as a natural part of growing up.

What Guides Us

Our Core Values

Compassion

We lead with empathy — every person we serve is treated with dignity and warmth.

Integrity

Every rupee, every relief kit and every promise is accounted for, openly.

Action

We move fast when it matters — hours count in an emergency, not days.

Community

Change is built together — with local people, for local people.

Our Journey

Milestones that shaped us

From one blood camp to twenty-five communities — each year taught us something new about service.

2019

The first camp

Founded by college students in Kozhikode; our first blood donation camp registers 80 donors in a day.

2020

Community welfare wing

Food and cloth distribution drives begin, supporting 400 families through the pandemic.

2021

Flood relief response

Our first coordinated disaster response — relief kits, shelters and rebuilding support for flood-hit villages.

2023

5,000 trees planted

Environment program crosses 5,000 saplings with school and coastal cleanup partnerships.

2025

25 communities reached

2,500+ volunteers now active across Kerala, with youth leadership workshops in every district we serve.
