Blood Donation Program

Blood Donation

A single unit of blood can save up to three lives. Our donor network connects hospitals and families with verified, willing donors — often within hours of a request.

  • Donor registration drives in colleges and workplaces
  • 24/7 emergency donor response network across districts
  • Blood group awareness camps and donor health checks

Community Welfare Program

Community Welfare

Hardship rarely announces itself. Our welfare wing runs steady, year-round support — food kits, clothing drives and education help for families working through difficult times.

  • Monthly food and grocery kit distribution
  • Cloth donation drives with dignity-first distribution
  • School kit and fee support for children in need

Disaster Relief Program

Disaster Relief

When floods or emergencies strike, hours matter. Our trained response teams mobilize relief kits, shelter support and cleanup crews — then stay for the rebuilding.

  • Rapid-response relief kits: food, water, medicine, essentials
  • Temporary shelter coordination with local authorities
  • Post-disaster cleanup and home rebuilding support

Environment Program

Environment Drives

We protect the Kerala we love — one sapling, one cleaned beach, one aware classroom at a time. Our green drives pair action with education so the change outlives the event.

  • Tree plantation drives with schools and residents' groups
  • Coastal and river cleanup campaigns
  • Waste segregation and plastic-free awareness sessions

Youth Development

  • Career and skill-development mentorship
  • Campus volunteer chapters in colleges across Kerala
